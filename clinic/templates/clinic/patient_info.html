Patient Information

Arrive prepared. Know what to expect. No second trip.

✅ What to Bring

  • National ID or passport
  • Referral letter, if available
  • Medical aid details / card
  • Previous X-rays, scans or reports
  • List of current medication
  • List of allergies
  • Previous operation records
  • Your questions for consultation

🩻 What Happens at Consultation

1. History — pain, injury, medical history
2. Examination — joint movement, walking
3. X-ray / Scan review
4. Clear plan + cost
5. Next steps — surgery, physio, or follow-up


After Consultation

You get a written plan, cost estimate, and referral for physio / tests if needed.


How to Prepare

Wear loose clothing. Bring someone to help you if you have hip/knee pain. Bring all old X-rays — don't throw them away. Write your questions down before you come.
