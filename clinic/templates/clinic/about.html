Dr. A. C. Mageza

Orthopaedic Surgeon | Adult Reconstruction Specialist

Dr. A. C. Mageza is a Harare-based orthopaedic surgeon and the principal practitioner at OrthoRecon, a specialist orthopaedic practice focused on the assessment and management of bone, joint, and musculoskeletal conditions.

With a clinical interest in adult reconstruction and orthopaedic trauma, Dr Mageza provides specialist consultations, treatment planning, and follow-up care. His practice is located at 2 Seagrave Road, Upper East, Mt Pleasant, Harare.

Dr Mageza is committed to evidence-informed care, clear communication with patients and referring clinicians, and appropriate specialist management of orthopaedic conditions.
