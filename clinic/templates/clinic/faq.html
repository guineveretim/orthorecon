Frequently Asked Questions

This answers most calls, so staff can focus on patient care.

Where is OrthoRecon located?

Harare, Zimbabwe. Full address & directions on Contact page.

What are your operating hours?

Mon - Fri 08:00 - 16:30. Sat by appointment.

Do I need a referral letter?

No, but it helps. You can book directly.

What should I bring?

ID, medical aid card, old X-rays, medication list, allergy list, previous operation records.

Do you treat hip or knee problems?

Yes — hip replacement, knee replacement, complex fractures, pelvic reconstruction.

How do I prepare?

Wear loose clothing, bring all X-rays, write your questions down.

How do I book?

WhatsApp or fill the Google Form on Contact page.

What happens after consultation?

You get a clear plan, cost estimate, and next steps.
